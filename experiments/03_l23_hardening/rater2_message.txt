THE FIVE THINGS

3. FUSED: separate objects melted or merged into each other, like a mix of two things.
4. WARPED: shapes that are melted, bent, twisted or physically impossible.

For the last one, answer just 0 or 1:
5. PATTERN: 1 if the whole picture has turned into a repeating pattern or texture instead of a readable scene. Otherwise 0.

Two special cases:
- Forest pictures: many trees is normal, so don't count the trees as copies. Score only trees that are fused, warped or broken, or a picture that has become a pattern.
- Oranges pictures: a bowl full of oranges is normal. Count copies only if there are far too many, or oranges spread outside the bowl or all over the picture.

Judge only what you can actually see, not what the picture was supposed to look like.
